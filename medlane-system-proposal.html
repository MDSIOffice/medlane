Core Features

FeaturePurpose
Role-Based LoginAdmin, CEO, Accounting, Sales, Logistics, and HR users only see modules relevant to their work.
Dashboard & AnalyticsVisualizes sales by branch, AR status, collection efficiency, top items, stock health, future growth indicators, and recommended actions.
Workflow AssistantShows module-specific task queues and recommendations so users can open urgent AR, low stock, missing documents, approvals, imports, and reconciliation checks faster.
MasterlistsCentral records for clients, items, suppliers, banks, and employees with duplicate client/TIN, duplicate item, duplicate bank, and document-completion checks.

Near-Expiry Sales AlertFlags reagents/items near expiry so Sales can prioritize movement before expiry risk increases.
InvoicingCreates SI, TS, and DRS layouts with automatic address, TIN, tax, net amount, item terms, FEFO lot autofill, exact-lot stock deduction, discount approval checks, duplicate document blocking, and credit-limit validation.
CollectionsRecords cash, cheque, bank deposit, bank transfer, partial payments, due dates, receipt numbers, cheque details, AR status, and payment-over-balance prevention.
Collection Contact MapDisplays a Philippines map using projected regional overlays, including NCR and CAR, with collection-status colors and clickable region/client follow-up queues.
Receivables TrackerGroups open AR by client and invoice stage with order-style timelines, status tabs, invoice drilldowns, and direct collection follow-up actions.
PayablesTracks supplier bills, cheque numbers, bank details, paid/partial status, payment voucher-ready information, and cheque-only field validation.
ReplenishmentsAllows petty cash and per diem requests with HR and Accounting approval flow.
Equipment Warranty TrackingTracks equipment serial numbers, installation dates, warranty end dates, service notes, and warranty status.
Client Purchase HistoryShows what each client bought, order count, lifetime value, AR balance, and last purchase date.

Import Safety CheckerChecks pasted CSV rows for missing fields, unsupported areas, and duplicate clients before import so unsafe spreadsheet rows are blocked.

Workflow Automation and Error Prevention

1Task Queues

Action queues show what to collect, approve, receive, reconcile, or complete next instead of relying on manual checking.

2Autofill

Client, item, payment, warehouse, FEFO lot, receipt, tag, and balance fields are pre-filled where possible to reduce repetitive encoding.

3Save-Time Validation

The system blocks duplicate documents, duplicate receipts, invalid cheque records, expired lots, missing lot/expiry, overpayments, and unsafe imports.
